Ireland Transport Decarbonisation Tracker

RoleRepository owner · Streamlit build · Data preprocessing and Forecasting notebooks

2026 · Group of seven

Asked
Is Ireland on track for its 2030 transport targets?
Found
No. EV uptake plateaus 18.4 points short, which works out at 1,860 extra EV and hybrid registrations every month between now and 2030.

Nine official CSO and data.gov.ie datasets, roughly 300,000 rows, pulled into one governed pipeline behind three composite KPIs. Forecasts run on Holt-Winters, SARIMA and logistic growth, selected per series and reported with error rather than presented as certainties.

Built for policy analysts and local authority planners, so the hard part was making forecasting output legible to people who will never open a notebook.

Customer churn prediction, telecoms

RoleData preparation · Model comparison · Interpretability

2025 · Group of five

Asked
Which customer segments are we losing?
Found
The demographics the business had been segmenting by barely mattered. Payment delinquency, short tenure and competitor pressure did.

50,000 customer records and 35 variables cleaned into 114 predictors. Six classifiers compared, XGBoost selected at 84 percent accuracy and 86 percent precision. SHAP and LIME applied so a flagged customer arrives with its reasons attached instead of a bare score, which is the difference between a model and something a retention team can act on.

Recommendation was continuous scoring inside the CRM, auto-pay incentives and intervention concentrated in the first six months of tenure.

Global supply chain optimisation

RoleData preparation · MILP build · Cost analysis · Recommendations

2026 · Group of seven

Asked
How much new capacity does the manufacturer need to build?
Found
Less than they thought. 20,000 of the 35,000 unit monthly shortfall could be covered by capacity already sitting idle, at a net saving of EUR 150 to 250 per unit.

A mixed integer linear programme across seven decision variable classes, with shortest path routing over a 15 node network, then stress tested with two-stage stochastic programming and a 500 iteration Monte Carlo so the plan came with a range rather than a single number.

The same model surfaced a 35,000 unit monthly capacity shortfall worth EUR 37.5M a year in penalties, against 20,000 units of idle capacity that could be switched on at a net saving of EUR 150 to 250 per unit.

  • Monte Carlo

Urban Moonlight, smart streetlighting

RoleIdeation lead · Prototype build · Testing framework

2025 · Group of six

Asked
How do we light Cork's streets better after dark?
Found
Visibility was not the barrier. Fear of falling, and of being alone if you did, was what kept elderly and disabled residents indoors. That turned a replacement project into a retrofit.

Stakeholder interviews, empathy and journey mapping with the residents the scheme was meant to serve. The requirements that came out of it were motion-activated lighting, an emergency call function with a cancel countdown to prevent false alarms, voice and braille accessibility panels and charging points.
